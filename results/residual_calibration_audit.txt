Calibration audit of the innovation target (Section 3.8.2)
n = 40 entities, N = 199 permutations, 400 replications, alpha = 0.05, cap log(N+1) = 5.298
An e-value is valid for the null it is pointed at.  With a FITTED map the null
is only approximately true, because the map's estimation error is itself an
F_(t-1)-measurable, entity-specific offset.  The oracle rows isolate the exact null.

Reading it.  Read E[logE] and E[R/(N+1)] first; E[E_t] is reported for continuity
with scripts/audit_calibration.py but is too skewed to read at these sample sizes.
On the oracle row of its own generator the innovation e-value is calibrated:
E[logE] <= 0 and the rank is uniform, as Lemma 3.3 requires.  The fitted rows sit
above it by the price of estimating the map, which is an F_(t-1)-measurable,
entity-specific offset and therefore a genuine (if small) failure of H_0^res, not
a failure of the estimator.  On the fixed_effect generator neither map is exact
and both innovation ranks sit below 1/2 on a panel where nothing is changing: a
pooled affine map cannot remove an entity fixed effect combined with
autoregressive dynamics.  What saves that case here is only that the candidate
is too weak to convert the residual structure into growth -- E[logE] is still
negative -- which is a statement about power, not about validity.  Compare every
row against the level row of the same block, which is what Sections 3.6 and 3.7
monitor.
